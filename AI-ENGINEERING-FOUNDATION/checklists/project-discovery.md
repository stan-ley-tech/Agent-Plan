# Checklist: Project Discovery

Use at the end of Workflows 01 and 02. A checklist catches omissions; it does not replace judgment. Mark items N/A with a reason when they do not apply.

## Problem and users

- [ ] The problem is stated in plain language and confirmed by the user.
- [ ] Target users and their primary goals are identified.
- [ ] Administrators, operators, and other affected parties are identified.
- [ ] The intended outcome is described in terms of what changes for users.

## Requirements

- [ ] Primary user workflows are written, including failure and cancellation paths.
- [ ] Functional requirements are numbered and traceable to a source.
- [ ] Nonfunctional requirements are limited to those that matter and are measurable.
- [ ] Business rules and invariants are explicit.
- [ ] Data sensitivity is identified (personal, financial, health, credentials, confidential).

## Scope

- [ ] In-scope items are listed for the first release or milestone.
- [ ] Non-goals are listed explicitly.
- [ ] Constraints (budget, timeline, technology, hosting, regulation, existing systems) are recorded.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Every functional requirement has at least one testable acceptance criterion.

## Unknowns

- [ ] Assumptions are labeled and listed for confirmation.
- [ ] Open questions list owner, impact, and whether they block progress.
- [ ] Blocking questions are answered or explicitly deferred by the user.
- [ ] Nothing was invented to fill gaps.

## Approval

- [ ] Initial risk tier assigned and justified.
- [ ] User has approved the discovery summary / specification.
